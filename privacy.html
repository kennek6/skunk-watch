What Skunk Watch collects

No accounts, no names, no email addresses, no advertising, and no analytics. The page is static and most of it runs entirely in your browser.

When you check conditions

Your coordinates are rounded to two decimal places, about 1 km, and sent to Open-Meteo to fetch the temperature and recent rainfall. Nothing about that request is stored by Skunk Watch. If you type a town name instead, the name goes to Open-Meteo's place lookup and your own position is never involved.

The activity estimate itself is calculated on your device. It is never sent anywhere.

When you send a report

The point you tapped is converted to a grid cell before anything is written down, and the original coordinate is discarded. There is no latitude or longitude column in the database. A sighting becomes a cell about 150 m across; a smell report, which could have come from much further away, becomes a cell about 1 km across. The map only ever draws cells roughly 900 m across, and draws the square rather than a dot, because a dot would put back the precision that was deliberately removed.

The counts that outlive your report look like this area, week of 2 November, 4 reports. They carry no timestamp, no device number, and no link to any individual report, so they are not about an identifiable person.

Why there is no "delete my data" form

There is nothing left to delete. Individual reports are gone within 48 hours whether or not anyone asks, and what remains afterwards is an anonymous count for an area that cannot be traced back to a device or a person. A request form would arrive after the data it referred to had already been removed.

What is never collected

  • Your exact location, at any point, for any purpose
  • Your home address, or any saved "my home" position. If you use the location button, the coordinates stay in your browser
  • Photographs. Image files carry exact GPS coordinates and a device identifier, and a photograph of a skunk also shows whose fence it is
  • Free text. There is no comment box anywhere in the app, because no amount of coarsening stops someone typing an address into one
  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact details

Other services involved

  • Open-Meteo receives a coordinate rounded to about 1 km, or a place name, for weather and place lookup
  • OpenStreetMap serves the map tiles, which means it sees which map squares your browser requests
  • Cloudflare hosts the reporting API; GitHub Pages serves this page. Both see ordinary web server request logs

A caution about the estimate

The activity estimate is built from published skunk behaviour but the weighting has not been validated against field data. The app warns when conditions favour activity and otherwise shows nothing. It never tells you that no skunk is nearby, because it cannot know that.
